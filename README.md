# Jñāna Yoga for Anxiety

A free, illustrated, practical guide for anyone living with anxiety, by Wes Gunderson (Yoga with Wes). One long scroll in fourteen chapters: real stories, the science in plain words, and tools that help in the moment — a guided 5-4-3-2-1 grounding, breathing pacers (box breath, 4-7-8), and a daily practice for every chapter.

**Read it:** https://allthebigideas-bit.github.io/yoga-for-anxiety/

- Two editions: Pink & violet (default) and Sea Glass, chosen from the contents menu.
- Sound is off until you turn it on. Music: “A Little Powder” by Blue Dot Sessions (sessions.blue), CC BY-NC 4.0.
- Your notes stay on your own device.
- Printable: [The Basics, 21 days](handouts/the-basics-21-days.pdf).

This repository holds the built website only. It is published from the claude.ai edition of the book, where it is written and edited.

This guide shares one person's experience and the practices that helped him. It is not medical advice; if you are struggling, please talk to a doctor or a mental health professional.
